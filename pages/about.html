About Property.pk

Pakistan's Trusted Property Marketplace

Property.pk is a modern online property marketplace in Pakistan, built to provide buyers, sellers, landlords, and tenants with a safe, simple, and trusted platform.

We strive to make buying, selling, or renting a property easy and transparent for everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to digitize Pakistan's real estate market and give people a platform where they can easily:

  • Buy a property.
  • Sell a property.
  • Rent out a property.
  • Rent a property.

Our Services

On Property.pk, you can:

  • Publish a property listing for free.
  • Connect directly with buyers and tenants.
  • Give your property greater visibility through Premium Listing.
  • Access Property.pk Professional Property Services if you'd like our team to sell your property, arrange a buyer, or find a tenant and assist with the rental process.

Transparency

Honesty and transparency are our top priority. Property.pk charges no commission on free listings. Service charges apply only to clients who use Property.pk Professional Property Services, and all charges are clearly mentioned on the website in advance.

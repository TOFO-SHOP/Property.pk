Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Your privacy matters a great deal to us at Property.pk. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your information. By using Property.pk's website or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information as needed:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Password (Encrypted Form)
  • Account Information
  • Property Details
  • Property Images and Videos
  • Property Location
  • Property Price
  • Messages sent through the website
  • Device Information
  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Operating System
  • Website Usage Information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide our website's services, such as:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Publishing property listings.
  • Connecting buyers and sellers.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Preventing fraud and fake activity.
  • Improving website performance.
  • Sending important notifications and updates.

3. Property Listings

When you list a property on Property.pk, information such as the property title, description, price, city, images, and videos may be shown publicly on the website so that interested buyers or tenants can contact you.

4. Information Sharing

Property.pk never sells your personal information to any third party.

We may share information only in the following situations:

  • When required by the law of Pakistan.
  • In the case of a court order.
  • When requested by government authorities.
  • For investigating fraud or illegal activity.
  • With official payment or technical service providers when necessary.

5. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance and user experience. Cookies are used solely to make the website better.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to keep your personal information safe from unauthorized access, misuse, or data loss. However, no system on the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Security Notice

Property.pk will never ask you for your password, OTP code, verification code, or account login details via phone call, WhatsApp, SMS, email, or social media.

If anyone claiming to represent Property.pk asks for this information, do not trust them — it may be a fraud or phishing attempt. Please report it to our official support team immediately.

8. Payment Safety

Property.pk will never ask any user to send payment to a personal bank account, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or any personal wallet.

If you use a paid service such as a Premium Listing or Property.pk Professional Property Services, all charges will be clearly mentioned on the website. Every payment should only be made through the official payment method.

9. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping their password and login information secure. If a user shares their password or OTP with another person, they will be responsible for any resulting loss.

10. Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to other websites or services. Property.pk has no control over the privacy policies or security practices of those websites.

11. Children's Privacy

Property.pk's services are intended only for individuals aged 18 years or older. If we learn that a user under 18 has created an account, we may remove or suspend that account.

12. User Rights

Every user has the right to:

  • Update their profile.
  • Edit their property listing.
  • Delete their property listing.
  • Request permanent deletion of their account.
  • Correct any incorrect information.

13. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal requirements, or prevent fraud.

14. Changes to Privacy Policy

Property.pk may update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. The new Privacy Policy will take effect as soon as it is published on the website.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Contact Us page.

16. Acceptance of Privacy Policy

Using Property.pk's website or services means you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.
